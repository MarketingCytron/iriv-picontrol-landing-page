IRIV PiControl

IR4.0 Industrial Controller

IRIV PiControl turns a Raspberry Pi Compute Module 4 or 5 into a DIN-rail industrial controller. Isolated digital I/O, RS485 Modbus RTU and Gigabit Ethernet in a metal case built for a control cabinet. Programmed in Node-RED, Python or CodeSys, so one box runs the machine, the dashboard and the cloud link.

Retrofitting a 1 MW Solar Farm When the Inverters Outlived Their Software

A 1 MW solar farm lost its monitoring when the Kaco Powador inverters reached end of support. Rather than replace them, EDA Solutions retrofitted IRIV PiControl: 16 inverters daisy-chained onto one isolated RS485 port, a custom Node-RED flow decoding the Kaco serial format, and MQTT feeding Grafana Cloud. AC power, power factor, current, voltage, energy and inverter status are live again, without buying a single new inverter.

An Industry 4.0 Training Kit for Robotics & Mechatronics Students

Built with Ir. Dr. Hudyjaya Siswoyo Jo for the Robotics & Mechatronics Engineering programme at Swinburne Sarawak. A PLC drives the actuators and sensors, an HMI shows live status, and IRIV PiControl sits between them as the IoT gateway, pulling production data off the PLC over Ethernet to cloud or local storage. Running OpenCV and Python, the same unit doubles as an edge processor for counting and basic quality inspection.

MyAgriTech Outgrew the ESP32 and Moved to IRIV PiControl

MyAgriTech’s IoT farm platform started on ESP32, but precision irrigation, soil health monitoring and remote sites pushed past what it could carry: data volume, on-device processing, and a stable link across a large farm. On Raspberry Pi CM4 it found the headroom. MyAgriTech now runs monitoring, smart irrigation that adjusts to weather and soil moisture, and automated fertiliser mixing across several farms, with a Node-RED interface for live local monitoring.

Real-Time Energy Monitoring on a Production Machine

Manual meter checks only tell you what was happening the moment a technician looked. IRIV PiControl reads voltage, line voltage, current, total power, power factor and frequency continuously from the machine’s meter, moves it through Node-RED, and puts live gauges and historical trends on a Grafana dashboard. Technicians can see when a current change started and compare it against normal operation, moving maintenance from reactive checks to earlier, data-led decisions.
